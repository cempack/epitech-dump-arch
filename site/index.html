Scroll through all 11 features ↓
01 / 11
  1. 01 · Workspace

    Every project in its place.

    Clones your repos into the right module, names them from the subject, and files every PDF next to it.

    epi
  2. 02 · Project skeleton

    Zero-chore C scaffolding.

    Makefile, header and guard, error helper, Criterion test and .gitignore, from one command.

    :EpitechNew my_ls
  3. 03 · Pool libmy

    Pull libmy on demand.

    Call any of your 35 Pool functions: it lands in lib/my with what it calls, and my.h and the Makefile follow.

    <leader>ca
  4. 04 · Live meters

    See limits before Banana.

    Lines, arguments and nesting depth counted as you type, Banana's reports on save, hints for what only graders check.

    epitech.nvim · VS Code
  5. 05 · Rule explainer

    Every rule, explained.

    The rule under the cursor: how severe, who checks it, a bad and a good example. All 42 in :EpitechRules.

    <leader>cE
  6. 06 · One-key fixes

    Fix Banana in one key.

    Convert to switch, split statements, pass by pointer, name magic numbers, extract a function. Only when the code still does the same.

    <leader>ca · Ctrl+.
  7. 07 · Tests & coverage

    Criterion right in your editor.

    Failed asserts at their line, and coverage in the gutter: green for lines the tests ran, red for the ones they missed.

    <leader>cT
  8. 08 · Project check

    The whole project at once.

    Banana on every file, the hints, the Makefile and tracked build products, sorted by severity. Add a pre-push hook and it guards git push.

    <leader>cB
  9. 09 · Pre-delivery

    Know before the moulinette.

    A fresh clone of what you committed: build, relink, Banana, authorized functions, tests and coverage.

    epitech-predeliver
  10. 10 · Memory

    Leaks, in plain words.

    Valgrind's errors, leaks and open files, at the line of your code that caused them. Or rebuild with --asan.

    epitech-mem ./my_cat
  11. 11 · Self-check

    Proof that everything works.

    Compiles a real Criterion test and a CSFML program, checks Banana and the editors, and prints the exact fix.

    epitech-doctor
